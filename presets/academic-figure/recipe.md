academic-figure: academic-talk style where figures and tables are the stars. For research talks, paper presentations, and lab meetings.

## Layout principles
- Split a results slide into figure 8-9 columns and interpretation 3-4 columns. The title is the conclusion sentence that the figure shows.
- Give every figure and table a "Figure N." / "Table N." caption (in Korean decks: "그림 N." / "표 N.") and a source (paper, dataset, experiment number) in the caption role.
- Put axis labels, units, and n on charts (D6). Among the compared items, paint only our method in accent.
- Put formulas and algorithms in as mono text or as figures supplied by the user, not as generated images.

## Slide types that fit this style
Result figure + interpretation, comparison table (best value in bold), method diagram (contain), limitations and next experiments (short list)

## Don't
- Decorative icons (I1), stock photos (I4)
- Charts without axes and a source (D6)
- Titles that only name a topic such as "Experimental results", with no conclusion (W4)

keynote-type: talk style that says one sentence per slide in big type. For tech talks, conferences, and keynotes.

## Layout principles
- Make at least half the slides "one sentence" or "one big number". Put the explanation in the speaker notes.
- Left-align titles. Do not center them (L4). Put a big sentence on the upper-left or lower-left third line.
- Show code large in the mono role, at most 10 lines per slide. Mark only the line you are pointing at with accent.
- Put screenshots in large and point at one or two spots with a callout (H3).
- Whitespace is the emphasis. Do not fill empty space with decoration (H7).

## Slide types that fit this style
One sentence, big number, code, annotated screenshot, before/after comparison (two columns, left and right)

## Don't
- Bullet lists back to back (3 slides or fewer per deck)
- Rows of icons (I1)
- Dark background + neon (C4)
